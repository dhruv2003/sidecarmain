        Local by default
        
Private by default.

        
Early ideas shouldn’t pass through an unfamiliar proxy. Sidecar keeps the gateway, the dashboard and the history on your computer.

      

      

        
Runs on your computer

Strict folder boundaries
Tools stay inside the workspace you chose. Personal files and system paths are out of reach.

        
Project-scoped keys

        FAQ
        
Quick answers before you start.

        
The questions that come up most before the first install.

      

      

        
Where do my prompts and history go?
Everything runs locally. Requests pass through the Sidecar gateway on your machine, history keeps metadata only, and prompts go to Codex through your own account session. Sidecar does not host or store conversation content.

        
Do I pay per request?
No. Sidecar is free and adds no per-request fees. Your Codex account supplies the model entitlement, so usage follows your own plan. Rate limits protect that quota.

        
Which models can I use?
Whatever your linked Codex account offers. There is no per-model setup, and latency and token counts are reported per request.

        
What if I hit a connection error?
Run “Test connection” in the dashboard. It tells you whether the local port, the upstream handshake or the sign-in is the problem. If you’re still stuck, open a GitHub issue with your OS version and the test output.
