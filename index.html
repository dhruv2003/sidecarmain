FAQ

Quick answers before you start.

The questions that come up most before the first install.

shield_lock Where does my data and prompt history go? expand_more
Everything runs locally by default. Requests route through the Sidecar gateway on your machine, request history keeps metadata only, and prompts are forwarded to Codex as a direct account session — Sidecar does not host, log, or store your conversation content.
payments Is there a usage quota or do I pay per request? expand_more
Sidecar itself is free to use; it adds no per-request fees. Your personal Codex account supplies the model entitlement, so billing follows your own account's plan. Rate limits protect your quota with a configurable safety bucket.
bolt Which models does Sidecar work with? expand_more
Sidecar dispatches to your linked Codex session, so the models available from your Codex account are what you can test — no extra setup per model. Model telemetry (latency, token counts) is reported per request in the dashboard.
support_agent What if I need help or hit a connection error? expand_more
Open an issue on the Sidecar GitHub repository with your OS version and the test output. For quick checks, run the dashboard's "Test Connection" — it shows whether the local port, upstream handshake, or auth session is the blocker.
